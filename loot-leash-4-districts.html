HOW TO STEAL
Grab coins — each one clips onto the chain dragging behind you.
Bank on green pads — hold 0.6 s to convert chain → points. Chain resets to zero.
Guards bust you on contact — the whole chain scatters as fading coins.
 Guards also stomp links off your tail as they run past.
Lasers trip the alarm and cut links — your chain tail trips them too.
COIN GUN — your new trick: fire a link off your tail as a bouncing bullet.
 Bullets ricochet once, stun guards, disable turrets, and cut a rival's chain.
 Spent bullets land as pickable coins — reclaim your ammo.
Sentinel turrets shoot back (faster when alarmed). Whip or shoot them offline.
Greed Gate — the treasure room opens only while a chain is ×8 or longer.
 Gold bars inside are worth 3 links each. High risk, huge reward.
Vault Quake — every 16–22 s the purple crates rumble, then slide to new spots.
 The layout changes mid-match on top of being regenerated every single run.
RELIC LINKS — rare pickups that live IN your chain, tail-first at risk:
 Magnet pulls coins to it · Ghost shields the tail from lasers ·
 Mirror reflects turret bolts · Keg explodes when snapped or fired!
RIPENING — links glow amber over 20 s, worth up to double at full ripeness.
LASSO — cross your chain over itself to close a loop: coins inside are
 captured instantly, guards inside get hog-tied into a prisoner link (6× value).
NERVE — near-misses build a bank bonus up to +100%. Any hit wipes it.
REPO MAN — hit ×15 and an unstoppable collector hunts your tail,
 eating a link every 1.4 s. He phases through walls. Bank to banish him.
THE BURN — at 0:00 unbanked chains ignite at the tail, one link per second.
 Your remaining score is a lit fuse. Sprint to a pad!
THE TRANSFER RULE — shoot your rival's body and your fired coin
 joins THEIR chain. Hit their chain to cut it instead. Aim decides who profits.
CAMERAS — cops can only arrest you while a camera has SPOTTED you
 (or you tripped a laser). Stay out of the red cones, or shoot/whip cams down.
WIRE DOOR — a locked room with a panel outside. The wires cycle a cutter:
 press F (or ITEM) when it's on the hinted color. Wrong wire = shock + WANTED.
WEAPONS — grab grenades (G to throw), landmines (Q to plant, they arm in 1.2s),
 and 3 scattered GOLDEN CANNON parts — assemble all 3 for 5 free piercing megashots.
MATERIALS — glass walls shatter when shot (the noise makes you WANTED),
 oil slicks make you drift, neutral mines dot the floor.
FOUR DISTRICTS — every vault is split into themed wings that reshuffle each match:
 MARBLE HALL: round pillars — bullets ricochet off them at angles.
 SERVER FARM: tesla nodes arc electricity — touching it zaps a link + WANTED.
 BOILER DECK: steam vents hiss, then BLAST — knockback + a link torn off.
 FLOODED WING: water slows everyone... and douses THE BURN —
 a burning chain stops losing links while its tail is submerged!
CONVEYORS — belts carry you, loose coins, and grenades. Watch loot drift away.
WOODEN CRATES — 2 shots or a whip crack breaks them open. Coins spill out.
